The [Lab](https://lab.russvaccaro.com/) is where I build focused browser tools.
Each project starts with a narrow problem and turns it into a repeatable workflow.

## [Interview Drill](https://lab.russvaccaro.com/interview-drill/)

**Problem:** Interview practice can be unstructured, making it hard to improve a
specific answer through repetition.

**Built:** A focused drill for practicing interview responses.

**Learned:** A narrow practice loop makes it easier to repeat, review, and refine
an answer than a broad preparation checklist.

## [Local Scratchpad](https://lab.russvaccaro.com/local-scratchpad/)

**Problem:** Daily notes need a low-friction home without turning every thought
into a document-management task.

**Built:** A local scratchpad organized around daily pages.

**Learned:** Reducing setup and navigation matters as much as adding features to
a tool meant for everyday capture.

## [Learning Cards](https://lab.russvaccaro.com/learning-cards/)

**Problem:** Saving information is not the same as being able to recall it.

**Built:** A card-based workflow for revisiting material over time.

**Learned:** A useful learning tool should make retrieval practice simple, not
just make storage convenient.

## [Reading Log Explorer](https://lab.russvaccaro.com/reading-log-explorer/)

**Problem:** A chronological reading list makes it difficult to explore themes
and connections across books.

**Built:** An interactive way to browse a reading log.

**Learned:** Structured metadata becomes valuable when the interface helps turn
it into useful paths for exploration.

## [MEDDPPICC Scorecard](https://lab.russvaccaro.com/meddppicc-scorecard/)

**Problem:** A qualification framework is less useful when gaps remain implicit
or are discussed only from memory.

**Built:** A scorecard that makes MEDDPPICC coverage visible and reviewable.

**Learned:** The value of a score is the conversation it creates about evidence,
risk, and the next action—not the number alone.

## [Account POV Builder](https://lab.russvaccaro.com/account-pov-builder/)

**Problem:** Account research can accumulate facts without producing a clear,
customer-relevant point of view.

**Built:** A guided workflow for organizing research into an account POV.

**Learned:** Constraints and prompts help separate a useful commercial thesis
from a generic company summary.
